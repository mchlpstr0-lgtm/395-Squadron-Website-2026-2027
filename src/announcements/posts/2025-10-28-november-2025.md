**Report an Absence or Leaving the Program:**

If you are no longer attending cadets email: 395air@cadets.gc.ca to let us know. This will start the process of removing you from our database. If you are absent, email (395air@cadets.gc.ca) or text (780-619-9756) the Squadron to let us know. You need to include Rank, First and Last Name and the Date you are absent for. Uniforms can be returned on a Tuesday evening during regular training hours.

- Open to all cadets. Dress in sports gear.

**Nov 7-9, 2025: Field Training Exercise**

- Final list of cadets able to attend has been posted on TEAMS.
- Cadets attending to be dropped off at Jefferson Armouries (11630 109 St - far side of building) at 1700 Hrs (5pm). Cadets are to eat supper prior to being dropped off. Cadets should be wearing their outdoor clothing (coat, toque, gloves) upon drop off.
- Majority of training is outdoors so ensure you have extra socks, gloves, etc. to keep warm and change as they may get wet through out the day.

**Nov 10, 2025: NO Band Training**

**November 11: Remembrance Day Parade**

- Remembrance Day Parade on Nov 11 is mandatory for all cadets to attend. 395 RCACS goes to a couple of locations and the cadets have been split as per the following:
- **#1 T-Bird Flight will be at Norwood Legion** (11150 82 St). Arrival is 0930 Hrs. Pick up time is approx. 1130-1145am.
- **Selected Cadets that were confirmed are at Kipness Centre.** Timings and details were provided directly to the selected cadets assigned to this location.
- **All other cadets are at Beverly Cenotaph. Cadets to arrive at Maranatha Christian Reformed Church (11905 47St) for 9am**. There is a short non-denominational service at the church prior to marching to the cenotaph.
- Those joining for the marching to the cenotaph and ceremony arrive at the church for 930-945am.
- Cadets at Beverly Cenotaph can be picked up at the Cenotaph upon completion of the ceremony at approx. 1130-1145am.

- Parents/Guardians are welcome to attend the ceremonies as well.
- Dress for Remembrance Day Parade at all locations is the same as the picture above with poppy. Cadet Parka or outer wear is authorized. For new cadets who only have the FTU (green) Uniform it can be worn on Nov 11 with wedge or beret only. If you don’t have either of these uniforms, black pants and white shirt to be worn. Ensure that you are wearing close toed shoes with some grip for outdoor marching.

· **There will be no regular training this evening – just the parade to participate in.**

**Announcement from Supply:**

**Returning Cadets:**

- Bring in all cleaned/washed uniform parts needing exchanges to the unit on Tuesday nights to Supply for new parts.
- Go to the Supply Area of the website and Teams to get information on how to care for and wear your uniform.

**New Cadets:**

- You will start to get your Training Uniform mid-October. Supply staff will gather those who need a uniform on a training night. There is no need to bug supply – they will come and get you when it is your turn to have your uniform issued.

**COMMANDING OFFICER’S UPDATE**

**395 Griffon Squadron Cadets, Staff, Parents and Families**

Welcome back, cadets, staff, and families, to the start of another exciting training year at 395 Squadron. I hope everyone had a safe and enjoyable summer. Many of our cadets attended summer training across the country, and we are proud of the outstanding success and professionalism they demonstrated. You continue to show the best of what our squadron has to offer.

This year is a very special one for 395, as we celebrate our 80th anniversary. For eight decades, generations of cadets and staff have built a legacy of leadership, service, and excellence. Throughout this training year, we will honour that history with events that will bring together our current cadets and our alumni, connecting past, present, and future.

As we look ahead, I challenge each of you to set goals for yourself, step outside of your comfort zone, and take every opportunity that comes your way. Whether it’s learning new skills, developing leadership, or supporting your fellow cadets, remember that growth happens when you push yourself further than you thought you could go.

The year ahead is filled with opportunity. Let’s honour our past by making this year one that future cadets will look back on with pride. I’m excited to see what we will achieve together.

Succeed as One!!!

Per ardua ad astra – through adversity to the stars.

Capt D.L. Smart, CD,MA

Commanding Officer, 395 RCACS
