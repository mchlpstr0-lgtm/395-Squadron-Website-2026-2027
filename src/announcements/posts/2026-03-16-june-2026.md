**Report an Absence or Leaving the Program:**

If you are no longer attending cadets email: 395air@cadets.gc.ca to let us know. This will start the process of removing you from our database. Uniforms can be returned on a Tuesday evening during regular training hours.

**COMMANDING OFFICER’S UPDATE**

**395 Griffon Squadron Cadets, Staff, Parents and Families**

As we close another incredible cadet training year, I want to take a moment to congratulate all of our cadets on a fantastic Annual Ceremonial Review. You represented the squadron with professionalism, pride, and confidence, and your accomplishments this year are something to celebrate. Whether through training, leadership, community service, flying, marksmanship, effective speaking, music, drill, karate, or simply showing up and giving your best, each of you has contributed to the success of our squadron.

Thank you to our dedicated staff, whose countless hours, expertise, and commitment make opportunities like these possible for our cadets. Your passion for developing young people continues to make a lasting impact.

To our Squadron Sponsoring Committee, Air Cadet League volunteers, families, and friends…thank you for your unwavering support throughout the year. The success of our squadron is truly a team effort, and we are grateful for everything you do behind the scenes.

A special thank you to Maj Brown, Commanding Officer of 417 Domestic Response Squadron, for once again serving as our Reviewing Officer. We are grateful for your continued support of our squadron over the years and for the encouragement you have shown our cadets and staff alike. As you prepare for your upcoming Change of Command, please know that you will be missed. We wish you every success in your next chapter and hope you know you are always welcome back to visit.

Congratulations again to all of our cadets on a remarkable year. Enjoy your summer/summer camps, and I look forward to seeing what we accomplish together in the year ahead.

Succeed as One!!!

Per ardua ad astra – through adversity to the stars.

Capt D.L. Smart, CD,MA

Commanding Officer, 395 RCACS
