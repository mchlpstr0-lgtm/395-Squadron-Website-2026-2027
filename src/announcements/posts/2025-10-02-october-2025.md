**NEW CADETS - UNIFORM DAY!**

You will be issued your green Training Uniform on Saturday 18 October during a scheduled 1 hour timeslot. Your timeslot can be found on the corkboard at the ship on Tuesday nights and in the supply area on Teams.

You will be trying on your uniform over your clothing, so please wear a t-shirt, shorts or tight leggings, socks and runners. Parents and Guardians can wait for you in the parking lot as you will be going through several stations where staff members will be sizing you for the appropriate fit of the uniform.

You will be issued your blue Service Dress uniform in couple of months on a regular Tuesday night during a one-on-one appointment with supply staff. There are over 75 new cadets and the Service Dress uniform takes time to size, so be patient as it will take several months to issue everyone their blue uniform. Supply staff will find you when your turn comes up for kitting.

Visit the Supply Page for more uniform information: [Supply — 395 Edmonton Royal Canadian Air Cadet Squadron](/supply)

**Announcement from Supply:**

**Returning Cadets:**

- September is dedicated to uniform exchanges. After September there will be no more exchanges until December at the earliest.
- Try on your Blue and Training Uniform, including headdress and boots. Ensure you have all of your uniform parts, they fit, and they are serviceable.
- Wash both of your uniforms before cadets starts on 2 Sep.
- Bring in all cleaned/washed uniform parts needing exchanges to the unit on Tuesday nights in September to Supply for new parts.
- Go to the Supply Area of the website and Teams to get information on how to care for and wear your uniform.

**New Cadets:**

- You will start to get your Training Uniform mid-October.
- The exact date and time for your uniform issue will be in the order your joining paperwork is completed and validated.
- Until you receive your uniform you will wear a white collared shirt, black pants (no sweats or gym style clothes), and sturdy shoes. You will also need to bring a pen/pencil and notebook. Please talk to your Level Officer if you have questions about the dress expectations.
- Ongoing correspondence will be done through your Cadets 365 account.### **COMMANDING OFFICER’S UPDATE**

**395 Griffon Squadron Cadets, Staff, Parents and Families**

Welcome back, cadets, staff, and families, to the start of another exciting training year at 395 Squadron. I hope everyone had a safe and enjoyable summer. Many of our cadets attended summer training across the country, and we are proud of the outstanding success and professionalism they demonstrated. You continue to show the best of what our squadron has to offer.

This year is a very special one for 395, as we celebrate our 80th anniversary. For eight decades, generations of cadets and staff have built a legacy of leadership, service, and excellence. Throughout this training year, we will honour that history with events that will bring together our current cadets and our alumni, connecting past, present, and future.

As we look ahead, I challenge each of you to set goals for yourself, step outside of your comfort zone, and take every opportunity that comes your way. Whether it’s learning new skills, developing leadership, or supporting your fellow cadets, remember that growth happens when you push yourself further than you thought you could go.

The year ahead is filled with opportunity. Let’s honour our past by making this year one that future cadets will look back on with pride. I’m excited to see what we will achieve together.

Succeed as One!!!

Per ardua ad astra – through adversity to the stars.

Capt D.L. Smart, CD,MA

Commanding Officer, 395 RCACS
